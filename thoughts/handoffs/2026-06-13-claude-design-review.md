## Review summary

Claude Design scored the site on spatial composition only (colors, type, motion were locked). Primary finding: **too much unearned void** — the hero, featured projects, and footer all allocate dead space that reads as incomplete rather than restrained.

### What's working

- **All-work list** — the strongest section. Tight, scannable, information-dense. The cursor-follow preview is the "selective surprise" done right.
- **About section split** — left statement / right body is sound. Fixable with vertical alignment, not restructuring.
- **Mobile project cards** — full-width image edge-to-edge, text with margins below. Strong mobile pattern.

### What's not

Four criticals, five warnings.

---

## Criticals (must fix)

### C1. Hero is 60% unoccupied void

The FluidGlass shader was deleted in the redesign but the hero layout still assumes a generative background filling the top zone. Content sits in the bottom-left quadrant — the top 60% is featureless dark space. On mobile, 65% is pure black before any content appears.

**Fix:** Change hero from `justify-content: flex-end` to `justify-content: center` (or top-40%). The name should sit at the gravitational center with space radiating outward, not sink to the floor. Works whether a future generative piece loads or not.

**CSS:** `.hero { justify-content: center; }` and adjust `padding-bottom`.

### C2. Featured project entries all have identical spatial DNA

All three use the same two-column template (text ~40%, image ~55%, sides alternating). The text columns are half-empty — content occupies ~40% of the vertical height, the remaining 60% is dead space. Violates "varied rhythm over uniform grids."

**Fix:** Give at least one entry a structurally different layout (full-width image with text below, metric-led, or reduced-width). Vertically center text against images so dead space distributes evenly.

### C3. FerroML data plate is an orphan

The box-plot visualization takes an entire viewport between P/01 and P/02 with no visible parent. A thin rule separates it from nspb-api above; the "02" number appears below it. A fast-scanning visitor can't tell what it belongs to.

**Fix:** Anchor the chart inside FerroML's entry boundary. Move project context (title, label) above the viz, or nest it within the entry's visual boundary.

### C4. Footer dissipates instead of concluding

Three problems:
1. **Timeline is centered** while everything else is left-aligned — spatial logic breaks
2. **Giant scrambled "TREY LUPO"** at ~8-10rem is the second display-scale name on the page — redundant
3. **Footer is a single line** of small mono text — doesn't invite action

**Fix:** Fold timeline into the about section (right column, below skills). Kill or dramatically shrink the scrambled-text. Expand footer into a proper conclusion: closing statement at body size, generous gap, then functional links with the email at readable size. The footer should feel like a door held open, not a curtain dropping.

## Warnings (should fix)

### W1. Nav vertical stack is the loudest structural chrome

Four links stacked vertically on the left (PROJECTS / ABOUT / CONTACT / RESUME) is unusual and reads as a sidebar that landed in the header. It's the loudest piece of infrastructure on the page — the exact thing that should disappear.

**Fix:** Flatten to a horizontal row (`projects · about · contact` at mono label size), or collapse behind hamburger on all viewports. Remove "RESUME" from the hero nav (move to about section or footer).

### W2. Two CTAs in the hero

"View work" (ghost) + "Get in touch" (signal blue) side by side. The ghost button is redundant with scrolling and with the PROJECTS nav link. Dilutes signal blue's rarity.

**Fix:** Remove "View work" entirely. One button: Get in touch. The content below the fold is the scroll invitation.

### W3. Hero → Featured Work has no structural bridge

No marker, tonal shift, or spatial breath between the hero and the first project. The transition feels unmarked.

**Fix:** Add a deliberate transition — 128px void with a `border-faint` horizontal rule, or a `surface-deep` background shift for the featured work band.

### W4. Project index numbers are in no-man's-land

~4.5rem at opacity 0.3 — too big to be metadata, too transparent to be a design feature. Ghostly rather than structural.

**Fix:** Commit to one direction: either bold structural device (full opacity, 6-8rem, oversized glyph) or quiet metadata (`p/01` at 0.7rem mono label).

### W5. About section decorative arcs

Circular/arc shapes in the upper right are the only decorative geometric elements on the entire page. They don't connect to the grid, content, or any visual motif. Frame asserting itself.

**Fix:** Remove entirely. The statement at display scale and skill density provide sufficient visual interest.
